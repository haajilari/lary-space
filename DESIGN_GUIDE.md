# LarySpace — Visual identity

**Brand source of truth:** `public/branding/laryspace-logo-original.png` (unaltered original provided by owner).

The attached transparent logo contains a dark evergreen wordmark and a richer emerald gradient in its symbol. To preserve legibility, the original dark lettering appears on cream/ivory surfaces, not directly on the dark cinematic hero.

## Palette

| Token | Hex | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| `--night` | `#061B14` | Main storytelling canvas |
| `--green-deep` | `#023C28` | Forest depth / projects |
| `--green` | `#049660` | Buttons, active paths, important accents |
| `--green-soft` | `#B5DDBD` | Subtle accents |
| `--paper` | `#F2F4EC` | Logo background and editorial sections |
| `--ink` | `#112F22` | Text on light panels |

These are design companions sampled and curated around the original PNG, **not a replacement logo**. Source alpha is retained in copies.

## Motion principles

- Motion should reveal meaning rather than be decoration.
- Shell fragments represent personal growth: a small central crack, side shell separating, top/bottom last.
- Leaving sections fade softly rather than vanishing while being read.
- Motion is reversible when scrolling back and disabled for `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Typography

- Latin: Avenir Next / system UI fallbacks.
- Persian: Vazirmatn variable font (weights 100–900), bundled in `app/fonts/` from upstream v33.003 and self-hosted through `next/font/local`; Vazir / Tahoma / sans-serif fallbacks. The SIL Open Font License is included beside the font. No font CDN is contacted at runtime.
- Precise lettering and generous whitespace; avoid overloading the page with effects.

## Editorial tone

Technical credibility plus personality. The portfolio still identifies its owner as **Software Engineer**, prioritizes **Python · TypeScript · JavaScript**, and clearly separates engineering work from personal Soundspace.
